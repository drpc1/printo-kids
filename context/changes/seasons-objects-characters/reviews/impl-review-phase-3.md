## Findings

### F1 — Hat, igloo and scarf added to COLOR_VARIANT_OBJECTS

- **Severity**: ⚠️ WARNING
- **Impact**: 🔎 MEDIUM — real tradeoff; pause to reason through it
- **Dimension**: Pattern Consistency
- **Location**: src/lib/symmetry/generate.test.ts:26
- **Detail**: In black, the variants of these three objects differ mostly by color (stripes, cuff, door frame), so their black silhouettes repeat. On 9×24 the igloo and the scarf have two parts instead of three. The mockup check did not cover these two rules. The exemption follows the Christmas precedent (bombka, prezent); the colored pictures are still distinct. The cost is that in black (nonogram, black „Skopiuj obrazek”, symmetry without colors) these objects give fewer distinct pictures. The category tests still require at least two distinct cards in every combination, and they pass.
- **Fix A ⭐ Recommended**: Keep the exemption, as for bombka and prezent.
  - Strength: The mockup was accepted as drawn, and there is precedent in this test.
  - Tradeoff: Fewer distinct black pictures for three objects.
  - Confidence: HIGH — the category tests pass with at least two cards.
  - Blind spot: How often black cards repeat in practice.
- **Fix B**: Add a third part with a different silhouette (e.g. a wider knot on the scarf, split igloo bricks).
  - Strength: Full compliance without an exemption.
  - Tradeoff: Changes the accepted drawings and needs another approval.
  - Confidence: MED.
  - Blind spot: Readability of the new variants in 4×12.
- **Decision**: PENDING
